A fairer start

Our mission is that from 2030 every child has the same chance of developing to their full potential in the early years.

The Challenge

The early years of a child's life are when the foundations of physical, emotional and social development are laid, but babies and young children growing up in poverty are more likely to miss out on the resources and experiences needed for healthy development.

This means they are less likely to live long, fulfilling and happy lives than their peers.

Growing up in poverty does not have to have such a powerful impact on life chances. As well as reducing child poverty, there are many protective factors, both inside and outside of the family unit, that can make sure every child has an opportunity to fulfil their potential, regardless of their family background.

The Goal

Our work in this mission spans four areas of focus to achieve our goal of every child in the UK having the same chance of developing to their full potential in the early years.
